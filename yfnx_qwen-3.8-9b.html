About YouFishNZ

Kia ora! I'm a dedicated tropical fish breeder based in New Zealand, passionate about raising healthy, vibrant angelfish and maintaining a small but carefully selected range of livebearers. My focus is on colour morphs with strong structural blue iridescence — the Philippine Blue line (Silver, Blue Platinum & Ghost), Pinoy & Black for their deep dark tones, and Bulgarian genetics including Seal Point and Green varieties.

I also offer a selection of hardy livebearer species alongside cultured micro worms to support your tank's ecosystem. My husbandry approach prioritises stable water quality, ethical breeding practices, and transparent sourcing — every fish is raised with care for its long-term health and temperament.

Philippine Blue Angelfish

Silver, Blue Platinum & Ghost — these display a striking structural blue iridescence that shifts with the angle of light. The Silver variant carries subtle grey-blue sheen across the dorsal surface; Blue Platinum shows deeper cobalt reflections on the fins and flanks; Ghost presents a lighter, almost ethereal wash of pale azure.

  • Silver — soft silver base with iridescent blue highlights that shimmer when swimming. Best viewed in dim lighting or against dark backgrounds.
  • Blue Platinum — intense cobalt reflections across the body and fins, particularly strong along the dorsal margin and anal fin edges.
  • Ghost — pale silver-blue wash over a lighter body tone, giving an almost translucent appearance in bright light.

Pinoy & Black Angelfish

Dark body morphs with rich metallic tones that deepen in low light. The Pinoy line carries a deep bronze-black hue with subtle copper reflections on the fins; Black presents an almost uniform obsidian tone across both body and fins, ideal for contrast against lighter tank mates or live plants.

  • Pinoy — dark brown to black base with warm metallic bronze highlights along the dorsal fin and anal margin. Fins carry a slight copper iridescence.
  • Black — deep obsidian body tone with minimal colour variation; fins are uniformly dark, creating a dramatic silhouette in planted tanks.

Bulgarian Seal Point & Green Angelfish

Specialty Bulgarian genetics — the Seal Point variant displays a classic seal-point pattern with dark markings around the eyes and mouth, while the Green line presents rich forest-green tones across the body. Both lines are bred for structural integrity and colour depth.

  • Bulgarian Seal Point — deep black mask around the eye and snout region with contrasting lighter body tones; dorsal fin carries a dark central bar.
  • Bulgarian Green — rich forest-green base colour with subtle blue undertones along the lateral line and faint iridescence on the anal fin edges.
