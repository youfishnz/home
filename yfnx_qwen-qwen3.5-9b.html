About YouFishNZ

Kia ora! Welcome to YouFishNZ — your trusted local tropical fish specialist in New Zealand. We specialise in premium angelfish, featuring stunning colour morphs including the Philippine Blue lines (Silver, Blue Platinum & Ghost varieties), Pinoy and Black dark body morphs with metallic tones, and exclusive Bulgarian genetics with Seal Point and Green variants. Beyond angelfish, we offer a curated selection of hardy livebearers such as guppies and swordtails, plus premium live food cultures like micro worms to ensure your fish thrive. Our husbandry approach prioritises water quality, selective breeding for vibrant colouration, and careful handling to deliver healthy, active specimens that will brighten your aquarium for years to come.
