About YouFishNZ

Kia ora! I've been breeding tropical fish for over a decade, focusing on maintaining vibrant colour lines and healthy genetics. My angelfish come from carefully selected Philippine Blue lines alongside other rare morphs. All fish are raised in NZ with premium live foods to ensure they're hardy and ready for your tank.

Angelfish

Premium colour lines from Philippine Blue, Pinoy, Black and Bulgarian genetics. Each fish is individually raised to maintain vibrant hues and healthy temperament.

NZ Based

Raised locally in New Zealand with strict biosecurity standards. All fish are quarantined, tested for parasites before sale, and acclimated to NZ water conditions.

Quality Genetics

Years of selective breeding have produced stable colour morphs that breed true. My lines are documented with generations tracked for consistent results.

Live Foods

I raise my own live foods including brine shrimp, bloodworms and micro worms to ensure fish get optimal nutrition before they reach your home.
