About YouFishNZ

Kia ora! My name is Younes. I've been breeding tropical fish for over 15 years, starting with angelfish colour morphs and expanding into livebearers and live foods. All my stock is bred in New Zealand — no imports, no hidden pathogens.

Angelfish: Philippine Blue lines (Silver, Blue Platinum & Ghost) with true structural blue iridescence; Pinoy and Black dark body morphs with metallic tones; Bulgarian Green and Seal Point specialty genetics. Each line is stabilised for colour intensity and temperament.

Livebearers: Guppies and swordtails raised in separate, disease-free tanks — perfect tank mates for angelfish once they're established.

Live foods: Micro worms cultured on-site for hatchlings and fry. No frozen alternatives — just fresh, nutritious food that boosts growth rates.

Philippine Blue Angelfish

Silver, Blue Platinum & Ghost. True structural blue iridescence — not just pigment. Silver base with electric-blue flash when angled; Blue Platinum adds a deeper sapphire sheen; Ghost is an almost transparent silver with faint blue shimmer.

  • Silver — classic silver body with vivid structural blue flash.
  • Blue Platinum — richer, deeper blue iridescence over the silver base.
  • Ghost — near-transparent silver with subtle blue shimmer.

Pinoy & Black Angelfish

Dark body morphs & metallic tones. Pinoy is a deep charcoal-black with subtle green-blue metallic flash along the lateral line; Black is an almost pure black with faint iridescent sheen — both stunning in dim lighting.

  • Pinoy — dark charcoal body with green-blue metallic flash.
  • Black — near-black body with subtle iridescent shimmer.

Bulgarian Seal Point & Green

Specialty Bulgarian genetics. Seal Point is a crisp black body with clean white markings on the head and dorsal fin — elegant contrast. Green carries a natural olive-green hue over the silver base, reminiscent of wild-caught specimens but bred for stability.

  • Seal Point — black body with precise white head and dorsal marking.
  • Green — olive-green tint over silver base, stable colour line.
