About YouFishNZ

Kia ora! My name is Kai. I'm a passionate tropical fish enthusiast based in New Zealand, dedicated to breeding and raising healthy, vibrant tropical fish with a focus on natural husbandry practices.

🌊

Angelfish Colour Morphs

Specialising in Philippine Blue lines — including Silver, Blue Platinum and Ghost varieties. Each fish is a living work of art, raised with meticulous attention to water quality, nutrition, and genetics.

🐟

NZ Based Genetics

Our breeding program is proudly run here in New Zealand, using carefully selected stock to maintain vibrant colouration and robust health. We're building a genetic foundation for future generations of tropical fish enthusiasts.

🧬

Quality Genetics

Every angelfish is selected from the top breeding lines, ensuring consistent colour expression and structural beauty. We invest time in each generation to refine our stock and pass on the best genetics to keepers across Aotearoa.

🌿

Live Foods

In addition to our fish, we cultivate live foods including micro worms and other nutritious supplements that support optimal growth and colour development. Healthy fish start with healthy nutrition from day one.
